About Our Club

The KUET Film Club is a sanctuary for movie enthusiasts on campus. We explore global cinema, analyze filmmaking craft, and share our passion for movies.

Club Activities

  • Movie Shows: Regular screenings on campus.
  • Weekly Discussions: Deep dive into plot analysis every weekend.
  • Movie Festivals: Annual grand festivals featuring diverse genres.
  • Campus Stalls: Pop-up stalls with exclusive posters and DVDs.
